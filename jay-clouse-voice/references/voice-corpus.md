## Derived stats (honest estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~14 words in essays and LinkedIn posts; spoken sentences run longer and loop back on themselves ("I hate that. I hate that. I hate that.").
- One-line paragraphs: roughly 35% of essay paragraphs and most LinkedIn lines; the first line of a post is almost always a standalone sentence.
- Hook-type frequency across the 29 written excerpts: confessional admission or transparent metric ~35%, two-camps framing (crowd vs. audience, attention vs. trust) ~20%, question to the reader ~15%, named creator as evidence ~15%, flat aphorism ~15%.
- Parenthetical asides: 1-3 per essay, carrying the caveat or the wink ("(myself included)", "(and a little biased in that direction)", "(some worked, some were a waste of time)").
- Caps for emphasis: one word per piece at most (HOT, FEELS, YOU, LOT).
- Exclamation marks: rare, 0-1 per essay, usually inside a remembered belief ("NFTs were the future!") or a permission line ("follow it!").
- Emoji: effectively zero in essays and the sampled posts (research notes one ⬆ beside a growth figure); hashtags: zero.
- Profanity: zero in the sampled corpus; strongest word is "cesspool" in a parenthetical.
- Hedges: frequent and deliberate ("I'm not speaking from a mountaintop here", "probably", "I'm actually not sure why", "neither is better or worse"); he refuses absolutes on purpose.
- Numbers: transparent and exact, then complicated in the next sentence: $101,666 → $23,300 → $78,400; ~66,000 subscribers; revenue down 8%, expenses up 5%; $300,000+ per year; nearly 10 years.
- Favorite openers: a first-person admission ("I lost a lot of money in 2021.", "I've been up since 3am."); a metric with a doubt attached; a question ("You ever feel like...").
- Favorite closers: permission then one directive, ending on a short aphorism ("indifference is death.", "The only way to do hard things is to do hard things.", "choose not to.").
- Spoken register (published transcripts): repetition for emphasis, enumerated points ("point number 1 of 9"), apology for the rant, "sensitive boy", "villagers"; same anti-hype stance, warmer and more rambling than the essays.
