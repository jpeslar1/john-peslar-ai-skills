## Structural notes for the writer

- He runs on named, numbered systems: the Content GPS, the founder content machine, the 80/20 ratio list. A post without a named system or a numbered list is off-voice.
- Colon-teaser line endings are a habit on X: a claim, then a colon, then the promise of a breakdown below.
- Two-sentence aphorism pairs recur: a flat claim, then a short command or reversal ("Excellence is a habit. Get addicted to it.").
- Specific numbers attach to claims: 2.5 million followers, 3 years, 10% a month, 100,000+ founders. Round unsourced numbers are off-voice.
- Section and post openers are frequently lowercase on X, even when the rest of the post capitalizes normally.
- He signs off blog posts by first name only: "Let's win together, Matt."
