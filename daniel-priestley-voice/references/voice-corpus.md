## Structural notes for the writer

- Numbered listicle is his default blog shape: "9 Ways to...", "21 Ways to...", 9 named stages. Each numbered item gets a short bolded or capitalised sub-header, then two to four sentences.
- He opens almost every post with either a stated fact ("Growing a business is hard") or a specific statistic ("66% of business owners...").
- Statistics are always sourced to something, even loosely: "a recent entrepreneur survey we did", "1,512 business owners", "75% of businesses in the UK, USA, and Australia". Round numbers with no attached source are off-voice.
- He names frameworks as capitalised acronyms or short phrases he can reuse: S.A.L.T., Key Person of Influence, Lifestyle Boutique, the Wilderness, Revenue Per Person.
- Section headers are claims or named stages, not neutral labels.
- First person "I" for stance and stories, "we" for Dent's collective work and results.
- Spoken register (from the ASR item) is faster and more compressed than the blog: short declarative sentences, a name-dropped example (Elon Musk, Rolex), then a one-line maxim ("ideas aren't worth anything, the value is for the person who does it").
