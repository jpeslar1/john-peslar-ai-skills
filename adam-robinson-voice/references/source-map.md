## Known gaps

- **No verified LinkedIn corpus beyond one post.** The canonical Aug 2023 post is the only LinkedIn item with a URL; every LinkedIn-specific claim in SKILL.md (one caps word per post, 0-2 hashtags, engagement behavior) is inferred from the newsletter register and the secondary style-analysis posts, not sampled from a batch of his actual LinkedIn posts.
- **Spoken register is transcript-only.** The podcast and interview excerpts (items 20-25 in the corpus) come from transcripts, not the original audio or video. Punctuation, pacing, and pauses are the transcriber's, not verified against the recording.
- **No X/Twitter or short-form video sampled.** If he posts on other platforms, that register is not represented here at all.
- **Corpus numbering restarts within the file.** The "Excerpts" section runs 1-25 and the "Email newsletter excerpts" section restarts at 1-20; see the numbering note added directly in `voice-corpus.md`.
- **Derived stats are estimates, not measurements.** The percentages under "Derived stats" (hook-type frequency, one-line-paragraph rate, stacked-punctuation rate) are the corpus author's honest read of the sampled material, not a systematic count across his full output.
- **Sample skews 2026, one 2023 LinkedIn post as the exception.** Most excerpts are from a roughly five-month window (Mar-Aug 2026). Confirm his register, product mix, and fight targets have not shifted before treating this as current.

## Pattern notes

### 1. Radical revenue transparency
Monthly ARR posts with exact figures, channel math, and headcount. The skill should demand the user's real numbers and build a recurring transparency series, not one-off flexes.

### 2. Tiny-team leverage math
Big revenue plus tiny headcount stated together as the whole argument. Use the pairing, never the revenue number alone.

### 3. The disclaimer flip
Flexes are immediately reframed ("This is not me bragging about my ability as a founder") to convert a brag into a teachable mechanism.

### 4. Anti-VC as moral case
The VC argument is about founder freedom and odds, argued with downside math and honest exceptions (Klaviyo). Spice with receipts, not vibes.

### 5. Public self-interrogation
Ethically gray moves become content by accusing himself first ("Am I a scumbag for this?") and reasoning in the open.

### 6. Punching up and loving haters
He picks fights only with bigger players and treats criticism as fuel ("Haters are good", "Punching up is a good thing").

### 7. TAKEAWAY discipline
Every piece closes with a stated lesson; product plugs live in a P.S. with a social-proof number.
