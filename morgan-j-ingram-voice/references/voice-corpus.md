## Derived stats (honest estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~9 words in posts (mean ~11); HubSpot columns run 12-18 words with the same one-idea-per-sentence habit.
- One-line paragraphs: roughly 70% of post lines are a single sentence ('I was fed up.', 'Try this out.', 'It works.').
- Hook-type frequency across the 15 post excerpts: labelled tip ('COLD CALLING TIP:', 'Prospecting Tip:') ~30%, staged objection in the reader's words ~20%, first-person failure story ~20%, question to the room ('Struggling with...?', 'Can I share a secret?') ~20%, analogy opener (working out, Kobe) ~10%.
- Named micro-frameworks with timings: PLA, PAQ, 10-30-10, 'wait 1 to 1.5 seconds', 'five personalized emails and five personalized videos', '50 seconds' videos; one framework per piece.
- Numbers: concrete and self-sourced (almost 76%, 5-10 videos a year, 20-30 meetings per month, first five seconds, 3 years); about one figure per post, several per column; outside studies almost never cited.
- Double-dot pauses ('..') in about a quarter of posts ('Yeah.. but I'm just an SDR', 'Hold up Morgan.. I don't have time', 'You are reading that correctly.. 5-10 a year'); true ellipses rare; em dashes only in HubSpot-edited copy.
- ALL CAPS for stress: one word per post (KEY, EVERYTHING, NO, NOT, DON'T, ASAP); one all-caps opener ('PRACTICE?!').
- Emoji: 1-2 per LinkedIn post, usually as a line-ender; set = 👇 👇🏾 🤷🏽‍♂️ 🤦🏽‍♂️ 😳 🙏🏾 🔑 ⏰; zero in HubSpot and 30MPC copy.
- Exclamation marks: 0-2 per post, at the staged objection or the shout ('you pay!', 'I have to talk to you!'); questions outnumber exclamations about three to one.
- Hedges: rare ('I believe', 'I don't think anyone here wants'); confidence is stated as data ('I am only reporting the data').
- Profanity: zero. Mocking a prospect or an SDR: zero. Politics: zero.
- Favorite openers: a label and colon; a quoted objection with an emoji; 'Can I share a secret?'; 'Here's exactly what I say.'; 'Its Morgan J Ingram here'.
- Favorite closers: a question to the room ('What would you add? 👇🏾', 'How are you practicing every single day?', 'So why wouldn't you try this?'), 'Try this out. It works.', or a P.S. offer ('DM me. I got you.').
- Spoken vs written: no transcript sampled, but most posts caption his own coaching videos, so the written cadence is already the spoken one (short declaratives, 'Hold up', 'folks').
