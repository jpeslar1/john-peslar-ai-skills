# Examples

Copy-paste prompts for the John Peslar AI skill library.

## Founder Strategy

```text
Use $chamath-strategy-coach to pressure-test this business plan.

Context:
[paste plan]

I want the hard read, the real bottleneck, the compounding bet, the kill list, and the next 7 days of action.
```

```text
Use $chamath-strategy-coach to run a personal operating system reset.

My problem:
[describe ambition, inconsistency, distraction, or status pressure]

Ask me the questions first, then give me a 30-day operating cadence.
```
