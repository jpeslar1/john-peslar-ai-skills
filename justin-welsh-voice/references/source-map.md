## Pattern notes

### 1. Systems over hustle

Every tactic is presented as a process or playbook. "No process" is his diagnosis for failure; leverage points are his prescription for growth.

### 2. Life-first business

Business lessons are carried by ordinary-life stories (small towns, breweries, family). The point is autonomy, not scale.

### 3. Anti-complexity

Growth by subtraction: fewer products, fewer channels, one niche, "go deep, not wide".

### 4. Proof by specific number

Oddly specific figures (44,716 followers, 158 residents) replace adjectives. If a claim has no number, he tells the story instead.

### 5. Calm authority

High certainty delivered gently. No dunking, no urgency, no hype. Critiques patterns, never named people.
