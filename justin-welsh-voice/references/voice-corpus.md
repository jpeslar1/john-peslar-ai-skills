## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~12 words (range 3-25; frequent 2-5 word fragments as beats).
- One-line paragraphs: ~70-80% of lines in LinkedIn/X posts; ~30% in essays (estimate).
- Hook-type frequency (estimate across sampled posts and teasers): "Most people" contrarian ~25%, one-line tension teaser ~25%, personal anecdote ~20%, negation list ~10%, confession-reversal ~10%, subverted proverb ~10%.
- Emoji usage: near zero in essays; zero observed in sampled LinkedIn/X posts. No hashtags observed. No ALL CAPS observed.
- Favorite openers: "Most people...", "I used to think...", scene-drop anecdotes.
- Favorite closers: mirror question to the reader, then the ritual sign-off "That's all for this week. / See you next Saturday. / Cheers,".
- Numbers: oddly specific figures used as proof (44,716 followers, 158 residents, \$15M) rather than adjectives.
