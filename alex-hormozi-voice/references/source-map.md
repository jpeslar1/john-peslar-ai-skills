## What the corpus rests on

## Known gaps

- **No excerpt pulled directly from his own X or LinkedIn account.** Every social-post item in the corpus (items 7-19) is sourced through a third-party aggregator, not fetched from x.com or linkedin.com directly. Wording could be re-typed, paraphrased, or dated incorrectly by the aggregator.
- **No item has a specific date.** Every X-post citation reads "circa 2021-2023" or carries no date at all. The research date (2026-08-24) is when the corpus was assembled, not when any quote was originally posted.
- **No spoken-register excerpt.** The Game podcast and his YouTube channel are listed as sources for long-form spoken register, but no excerpt in the corpus is actually drawn from either. The voice DNA's claims about spoken pacing are inferred from the written aphorisms, not sampled from audio or video.
- **No current-era (2025-2026) material.** All 20 excerpts date to the books (2021, 2023) or undated X-era posts believed to be 2021-2023. Nothing reflects how his register may have shifted as Acquisition.com scaled into a larger portfolio.

- **Book lines (items 1-6, 19, 20) cannot be verified without the books.** acquisition.com hosts no free chapters.

## Pattern notes

### 1. Aphorism as unit of content

His native format is the compressed rule: a claim under 15 words that survives being read flatly. The skill should end every piece on one.

### 2. Math over motivation

Claims are backed with arithmetic: value equations, volume math, exchange rates of time for money. If a piece has no number, it is not finished.

### 3. Definition reframes

He wins arguments by redefining the noun first ("a lead is a person you can contact"). The skill deploys this at the top of educational content.

### 4. Volume gospel

His default prescription is more reps: more calls, more content, more outreach. The skill uses this against trick-seeking questions.

### 5. Give away the paid thing

His CTA posture is anti-CTA: free books, free courses, "I have nothing to sell you". The skill mirrors this in lead magnets and closers.
