# Gary Halbert Source Map

Research date: 2026-09-09
Refresh cadence: every 12 months (the corpus is historical and will not drift; next: 2027-09)

## Rights position

The rights holder explicitly prohibits reproducing or selling even one sentence of the newsletters. This skill therefore builds its corpus from the site's own public homepage sales letter and from widely republished Boron Letters lines, and it will not add newsletter issue text. Anyone refreshing this corpus must keep that boundary. Read the archive to learn the voice; do not copy it into this repository or into any product.

## What was actually fetched

The homepage letter was pulled as raw text on 2026-09-09 and supplied items 1 to 13 of the corpus. The newsletter index returned 403 to a scripted request. Boron Letters lines were gathered from secondary sources and are labeled `documented`.

## Known gaps

- **No newsletter issue text, by design.** This is a deliberate rights decision, not an oversight. The consequence is that the corpus samples his web-era self-promotional register but not his long-form teaching register at full length.
- **No Boron Letters primary text.** Items 14 to 16 are from secondary summaries. The father-to-son intimacy of that book, which is a distinct and softer register from the homepage letter, is described in this skill from reputation rather than from sampled prose.
- **No historical direct mail packages.** The famous controls exist mostly as third-party scans of uncertain provenance.
- **Single-document web sample.** One page, however good, is one page.

## Refresh instructions

1. Read three or four newsletter issues in a browser to internalise the long-form teaching register. Take structural notes only. Do not paste issue text into this repository.
2. If a licensed copy of The Boron Letters is available, replace items 14 to 16 with correctly cited short quotations and drop the `documented` labels.
3. Confirm the homepage letter has not changed. It has been stable for many years but it is the corpus's only primary source.
4. Re-read the rights notice before adding anything.
