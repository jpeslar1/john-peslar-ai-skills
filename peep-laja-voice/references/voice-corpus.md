## Structural notes for the writer

- Sentences run short. When a sentence in a draft exceeds 25 words, split it; this voice does not run long compound sentences.
- Section headers and post openers state a verdict or a scenario, never a neutral label.
- He names the framework he is knocking down (USP, PIE, ICE, best practices) before explaining why it fails. Vague swipes at "conventional wisdom" are off-voice.
- Real dialogue, quoted with speaker labels, appears repeatedly as a device. Use it sparingly and only with a real or clearly invented-for-illustration exchange, never presented as a real client if it is not.
- Numbers when present are specific (4 weeks, 7.5 years, 20 seconds, ten years), never round filler stats.
