# Weekly Sales Loop

Make selling fit the user's actual week. Produce three named blocks and a small scorecard that distinguishes real progress from a full-looking CRM.

## Fit the routine to capacity

Use the chosen ICP/channel, current workload, working days/timezone, weekly sales time, live opportunities, and historical counts. If time is unknown, propose a clearly labeled starter schedule rather than assuming daily hours the user does not have.

Create **List Build**, **Work-the-List**, and **Reply Triage** blocks. Reserve a brief same-working-day triage window before allocating remaining time between research and outreach. Include call preparation, actual calls, and review time in the total. State duration and purpose for every block and verify that the total fits the available hours. For an operator, use stakeholder mapping, sponsor conversations, and decision follow-through.

Work-the-List includes individualized initial outreach and due follow-ups to eligible prospects. Reply Triage stops sequences on replies, handles interested buyers, clarifies concerns, respects later/no requests, and records the next step. Do not schedule unapproved sends or modify a calendar unless execution is requested.

## Define and reconcile the scorecard

Capture a reporting period, timezone, and cohort. The core deck scorecard is sent / replied / booked. Expand only enough to diagnose the user's bottleneck:

`Period | Qualified prospects added | Unique people first contacted | Message touches | Unique replies | Positive replies | Booked | Held | Qualified opportunities | Proposals | Signed | Paid`

Track next owner, action, and date for active opportunities. Reconcile duplicates, reschedules, cancellations, and deals that started before the period. All-time pipeline stock is not this week's conversion cohort. Separate a booking created this week from a meeting held this week.

For a defined first-contact cohort, reply rate = unique repliers / unique contacted people; booking rate = unique booked prospects / unique contacted people. Report sample size and maturation window. If the denominator is missing or zero, show `N/A` and raw counts. Do not infer performance from tiny immature cohorts or treat repeated touches as new prospects.

## Diagnose the earliest supported leak

- Few qualified prospects: fix list sourcing or reachable ICP.
- Many touches but little delivered/reachable contact: check channel access before copy.
- Delivered outreach without relevant replies: inspect list fit, trigger evidence, and opener together.
- Relevant replies without bookings: inspect qualification, ask, and follow-through.
- Booked without held: inspect confirmation and scheduling friction.
- Qualified calls without progress: inspect agreed next steps, authority, offer, and price.

These are hypotheses to test, not automatic diagnoses. Look for the actual correspondence or stage evidence before declaring a cause. Remove no-fit, refused, and stale-without-next-step records from the active forecast while preserving history.

## Deliver

- Weekly schedule with the three blocks, daily triage, and total hours.
- Scorecard with definitions, actuals, unknowns, and cohort notes.
- Prioritized active list with next owner/action/date.
- One bottleneck hypothesis and one experiment for the next week.
- Review date and rule for continuing or changing the experiment.

## Working agreement

Use context already supplied. Ask only for missing facts that change the next decision, preferably in one short batch, then make a useful first draft. Label assumptions and distinguish buyer evidence from your interpretation. Never invent interviews, prospect facts, results, testimonials, budget, or meetings. A target date is a planning goal, not a promised commercial outcome. Use plain sentences and normal hyphens in generated copy.

Create drafts and plans within the requested scope. Sending messages, booking meetings, publishing, changing a CRM, or committing the user to commercial terms requires authorization for that action. A request for advice alone does not authorize execution. Work from pasted notes if tools are unavailable; identify what remains unverified.
