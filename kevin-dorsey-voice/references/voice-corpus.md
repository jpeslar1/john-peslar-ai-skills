# Kevin Dorsey Voice Corpus

Research date: 2026-10-19

The excerpts below are short quotations from public posts, articles, and talks, included under fair use for style analysis and attribution. They are third-party content and NOT covered by this repository's MIT license.

**Provenance note.** Items 1-28 are verbatim. Items 1-24 are copied from the full text of 16 of his own LinkedIn posts (profile kddorsey3, 2021-2026), fetched logged-out from each post permalink with his spelling, typos, and both spellings of y'all intact; item 10 is a sponsored (#AD) post and is labelled so. Items 25-28 are verbatim from an official host-published Q&A transcript of a Salesloft REVstars interview (PatientPop VP era, c. 2019, recovered from the Wayback Machine), so they are his spoken words as the host transcribed them, not ASR. Items 29-30 are DOCUMENTED: lines attributed to him in a GTM Newsletter podcast write-up, transcribed and selected by the publisher, not by him. Nothing here comes from a quote aggregator. His Sales Leadership Accelerator newsletter has no public archive and was not sampled; his Live Better Sell Better podcast and his Exit Five appearance have no readable transcripts and were not sampled.

**Punctuation note.** No excerpt contains an em dash or an en dash; he connects clauses with ellipses, line breaks, and hyphens, and uses a hyphen for list bullets (item 22). The operator's copy rule still bans em and en dashes in anything generated in this voice; see the Punctuation Override in SKILL.md.

## Derived stats (honest estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~7 words in posts (mean ~9); the interview transcript runs longer, 15-25 words, with 'Right?' as a checkpoint.
- One-line paragraphs: roughly two thirds of post lines are a single sentence or fragment; the longest post paragraph in the sample is three sentences.
- Hook-type frequency across the 24 post excerpts: flat verdict or inverted claim ~35% ('Pick one: carry a bag or build a team.'), quoted objection then reversal ~20%, first-person story or rep shout-out ~20%, framework reveal ~15%, buyer-hat or personal-life frame ~10%.
- Parallel 'Not X. Not Y. Not Z.' or triad runs: in about a third of posts (items 6, 14, 17, 20, 22).
- Ellipses: his main connective; '...' appears in roughly a quarter of posts ('Duh... No.', 'won't work... It's ok.'). Em and en dashes: zero.
- ALL CAPS for stress: one or two single words per post (BUY, THEIR, NAILED, HAVE, LIFE); one full-caps staged objection ('BUT BUT BUT KD THIS TAKES TOO LONG!!!').
- Exclamation marks: 0-2 per post, clustered at closers and shout-outs ('SDRs for LIFE!', 'Hang on tight!'); zero in the teaching lines.
- Emoji: zero in the sample. Hashtags: zero apart from the #AD disclosure.
- Profanity: mild and rare ('Damn I'm glad I did it', 'chill tf out'); the research notes 'Shit.' in the spoken transcript. Never aimed at a named person.
- Numbers: round and few, one per post at most (700 happy customers, 100 dials, 70 dials, 90 days, 3x3, 10 to 15% lift); no decimals, no cited studies.
- Hedges: near zero in posts; 'I think' appears only in the spoken transcript. Verdicts are stated flat and then explained.
- Favorite openers: a one-line verdict; a quoted objection in quotation marks; 'Y'all, ...'; 'This post is about...'; 'Let me give you one of the most powerful...'.
- Favorite closers: a two-beat inversion ('Things don't calm down. People get better.'), then a coach's send-off ('Let's go.', 'Get after it.', 'I'm telling ya.', 'This is the way y'all.') or an offer to share the asset.
- Spoken register (official transcript): same flat verdicts, looser syntax, 'Right?' checkpoints, self-interruption, candid admissions ('I'm not an activity hound. I'm an efficiency hound.'), Checklist Manifesto cited by name.
