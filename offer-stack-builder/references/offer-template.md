# Offer Template

## Offer Canvas

| Component | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Who is this specifically for? |
| Pain | What is painful now? |
| Outcome | What measurable result do they want? |
| Mechanism | Why does this work better than the old way? |
| Deliverables | What do they receive? |
| Proof | Why should they believe it? |
| Risk reversal | What reduces fear? |
| Time to value | How fast do they see progress? |
| Effort reduction | What do you do for them? |
| Next step | What should they do now? |

## Bonus Mapping

| Objection | Bonus |
|---|---|
| I do not have time | Done-for-you setup, template, checklist |
| I will not know what to do | Loom walkthrough, SOP, office hours |
| This will not work for my niche | Niche adaptation worksheet |
| I tried this before | Failure diagnosis, teardown, reset plan |
| I cannot justify the price | ROI calculator, savings estimate, case study |
