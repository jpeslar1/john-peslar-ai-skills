# Offer Stack Builder

Use this skill to make an offer clearer, more valuable, easier to buy, and easier to deliver. Focus on real value, not fake scarcity or inflated bonus math.

Read `references/offer-template.md` when producing a full offer page or sales asset.

## Core Principle

A strong offer makes the buyer believe four things:

1. I want that outcome.
2. This can work for someone like me.
3. The path is faster and easier than what I am doing now.
4. The risk of action is lower than the cost of staying stuck.

## Offer Audit

Score the current offer 1 to 10 across:

- Dream outcome
- Specific buyer
- Pain urgency
- Proof
- Time to value
- Effort reduction
- Risk reversal
- Clear deliverables
- Pricing logic
- Next step clarity

## Build Workflow

1. Define the buyer and painful current state.
2. Define the measurable outcome.
3. Name the mechanism that makes the outcome believable.
4. List obstacles between buyer and outcome.
5. Add deliverables that remove those obstacles.
6. Add proof and risk reversal.
7. Choose pricing logic.
8. Write the offer in plain language.

## Rules

- Do not inflate bonus values.
- Do not use fake urgency.
- Name the actual mechanism.
- Make deliverables concrete.
- Reduce effort before adding more information.
- Tie every bonus to an objection or obstacle.
