## Derived stats (honest estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~8 words in LinkedIn posts and ~11 in Substack essays; fragments of 1-4 words are routine ("For now.", "Picked?", "Ban it. Burn it.").
- One-line paragraphs: roughly 80% of LinkedIn post lines are a single sentence or fragment on its own line; Substack essays mix one-liners with 2-3 sentence paragraphs at about a 50/50 split.
- Hook-type frequency across the 37 written excerpts: second-person accusation or command ("Your prompt sucks.", "Don't ask ChatGPT or Claude for their opinions.") ~35%, flip-then-colon ("But not for the reason you think:") ~20%, interactive or participatory hook ("Pick a number between 1 and 10.") ~10%, growth receipt with an exact number ~15%, contrast pair ("ChatGPT writers all sound the same. Claude writers sound like themselves.") ~10%, launch or announcement ("I am launching GPC.") ~10%.
- Numbered procedure: present in roughly 70% of the LinkedIn posts sampled ("Step 1.", "Skill 1.", "Prompt 5.", "1. Task"); sub-bullets use ☑ ✦ ✓ → and ↳, with ↳ carrying the consequence or example.
- Quoted prompts: about 1 in 4 LinkedIn how-to posts includes a full prompt inside quotation marks with [BRACKET] variables; Substack essays include several per post.
- Emoji: ♻️ on nearly every LinkedIn close (repost ask), 👉 before links, ✓ and ✦ as bullets, a 🪄 in one Substack title; none inside sentences. Hashtags: zero since 2024 per the research notes, zero in the sample.
- Exclamation marks: effectively zero across all 41 excerpts; emphasis comes from periods and one-word lines, not punctuation. ALL CAPS: one instance ("WORDS to IMAGES").
- Profanity: none; the ceiling is "freaking Tuesday" and "Your prompt sucks." Hedges: near zero in writing ("100%", "always", "every time"); "like", "you know", and "um" appear only in the spoken sample.
- Numbers: large, precise, and unrounded as receipts (331,689 followers, 〜900 followers/day, 500 → 75,000 followers, 3M impressions per week, 700k+, 1,000,000 people, 50x variations, 9 out of 10 people, 22 rules, 7 skills); step counts are exact.
- Parentheticals: about 1 in 5 excerpts carries a joke or aside in parentheses ("(properly)", "(not) kidding", "(Yes, I'm telling you to stop using the things I built.)", "(twice)").
- Favorite openers: a second-person accusation ("Your prompt sucks.", "Your AI knowledge stops at ChatGPT."), "Uncomfortable truth:", "Most people will never...", "I built an entire...", "Zero introduction. Straight into the 'how':".
- Favorite closers: a ♻️ repost ask with a reason ("so your network stops prompting from zero too", "to help others on Linkedin"), a numbered subscribe ladder ending "Become the 'AI guy' at work, forever.", or a flat two-line verdict ("Use it to find the 7. Then go somewhere else.").
- Spoken register (ASR sample): discursive and hedged, restates the key line twice ("Am I using it to be lazier or greater?"), same no-personal-opinion stance; written register is clipped and imperative. Use written for posts, spoken only for video scripts.
