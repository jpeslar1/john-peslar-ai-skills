## Extracted Mental Models

### 1. Business Ideas Are Everywhere

MFM repeatedly looks for overlooked examples of people already making money. The lesson is to start from real demand, not blank-page brainstorming.

### 2. Weird Beats Generic

Specific, odd, or unfashionable businesses often have clearer demand and less competition than generic startup ideas.

### 3. Copy the Pattern

Do not copy the exact business blindly. Copy the mechanism: customer pain, channel, pricing, market timing, supply constraint, or content loop.

### 4. Test Small

Strong MFM-style ideas usually have an immediate first test: call buyers, make a landing page, sell a service version, scrape demand, or manually fulfill before building software.

### 5. Operator Fit Matters

An idea needs to match the user's skills, assets, access, risk tolerance, and energy. A great idea for someone else can be a trap.
