## What the corpus rests on

Thirty of thirty-four excerpts are verbatim from channels he publishes under his own name: twenty from 2017 garyvaynerchuk.com essays that carry a staff-assist footer, three from unassisted 2025-2026 posts on the same site, and seven from his own X account pulled directly from the syndication timeline (plus five supplementary one-liners). Four are documented-grade ASR captions from a keynote on his own YouTube channel. Nothing comes from a quote aggregator. The essay dialect and the X dialect are both sampled, which matters because they differ; the spoken sample is thin but consistent with both. Confidence in wording is high for items 1-30 (with the team-assist caveat on 1-20) and moderate for 31-34.

## Known gaps

- **The 2017 essays are team-assisted.** Each carries "This article was powered by Colin Campbell" (or a Medium cross-post note). They are his ideas in his rhythm, edited by staff; label them as such when quoting and do not treat them as proof of his unassisted sentence-level habits. The 2025-2026 posts and all tweets are unassisted.
- **X sample skews 2018-2023.** The syndication endpoint returned about 100 posts weighted to the NFT era (2021-2022); no 2024-2026 tweets were captured. The giveaway and airdrop posts are dated and should not be templated.
- **No newsletter archive.** The weekly newsletter is email-gated and no public archive was found, so the newsletter register is unsampled.
- **Books not sampled.** Crush It!, Jab Jab Jab Right Hook, and Day Trading Attention have no public extract located; famous book lines must not be quoted as verbatim from memory.
- **Spoken excerpts are auto-captions.** No punctuation, profanity masked as [__], ASR slips ("Tik Tok", "I'm talk to you"). Use them for cadence and stance, not exact wording. Only one keynote was captioned.
- **No LinkedIn sample.** His LinkedIn posts are behind login and were not fetched; the LinkedIn playbook is inferred from the essay and X dialects.
- **Anything published after 2026-10-18 is unsampled.**

## Pattern notes

### 1. Indict, then exempt
The reader is addressed as a room of watchers ("none of you actually act", "98% of you are going to do nothing") and he positions himself as the one who did ("I started VaynerMedia at 34"). The Ghostwriter playbook opens on the indictment and requires the user's own receipt within two paragraphs.

### 2. The two-beat label
Lessons are compressed into X / Y pairs he repeats for years: macro patience / micro speed, make money / don't raise money, sales vs. branding, money in vs. money out. The Contrarian Take Generator uses the label as the title.

### 3. Spoken cadence on the page
Essays open with "Listen," or "So…", stack short declaratives, and use one ALL-CAPS word and doubled "!!" at the peak. The Ghostwriter edit pass deletes any sentence he could not say aloud.

### 4. Two dialects
The X dialect is lowercase, " .. " for a pause, "u" and "pls", hearts or a 💜 at the end, and blessings aimed at the reader. The Hook Creator returns an X variant separately from the punctuated essay or LinkedIn version.

### 5. Mortality and the receipt
"You're gonna die" and "one at bat" reframe time; a round autobiographical number licenses the advice. Both appear once per essay in the Ghostwriter template.

### 6. Warmth under the dare
Profane impatience with watchers sits beside "so much love to everyone who's trying" and a ranking of kindness over skill. The LinkedIn Content Expert playbook requires one gratitude or kindness line so the dare does not read as contempt.
