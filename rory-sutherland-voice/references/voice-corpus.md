## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~22 words, the longest of any voice in this repo; punchlines drop to 6 to 10.
- One-line paragraphs: rare (~10%); he writes in 3 to 6 sentence prose paragraphs.
- Hook-type frequency: aphoristic inversions and costed absurdities dominate his most-quoted material; institutional confessions next.
- Emoji, hashtags, ALL CAPS: zero. Parentheticals: frequent. Genuine questions: roughly one per extended passage.
- Favorite openers: "Why is it necessary to...", "The trouble with...", "It is perfectly possible to...".
- Favorite closers: short paradox punchlines that invert the setup.
- Register: educated British conversational, amused, concrete artifacts (trains, wine, taxis, flowers) anchoring every abstraction.
