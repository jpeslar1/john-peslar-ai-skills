## Structural notes for the writer

- The war story always carries counts. 800 dials, 0 meetings, 40 dials, 3 meetings, 300M calls, 3x.
- Scripts are visually separated and written as speech, never as a summary of what to say.
- The joke lives in parentheses. The body stays tactical.
- The piece ends in a checklist phrased as questions the reader runs on their own draft.
- Frameworks borrowed from podcast guests are credited by name and episode number.
- Farrokh writes the comedic story-led issues, Cegelski the structural ones. Both use the same skeleton.
