# Founder Content System

## Weekly Cadence

| Day | Content Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Point of view | Teach the market how to think |
| Tuesday | Tactical post | Give a useful workflow |
| Wednesday | Founder story | Build trust and relatability |
| Thursday | Proof or teardown | Show receipts |
| Friday | Lead magnet or video | Convert attention into owned audience |

## Raw Material Sources

- Sales calls
- Customer wins
- Failed experiments
- Product builds
- Internal SOPs
- Research notes
- Founder journal
- Support questions
- Before-and-after examples

## Post Shapes

### Lesson From Work

```text
I thought [old belief].

Then [specific event] happened.

What changed:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

Now I do this instead:
[new operating rule]
```

### Tactical Breakdown

```text
Here is the exact workflow I use to [outcome]:

1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

Steal the template:
[CTA]
```

### Contrarian POV

```text
Most people think [common belief].

I think that misses the point.

The real issue is [diagnosis].

Here is what to do instead:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...
```

## Repurposing Map

One strong workflow can become:

- 1 LinkedIn tactical post
- 1 newsletter issue
- 1 YouTube walkthrough
- 1 lead magnet
- 3 short posts from the mistakes
- 5 DM follow-up snippets
