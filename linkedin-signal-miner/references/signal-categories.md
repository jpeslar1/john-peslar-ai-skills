# Signal Categories

## High-Intent Signals

| Signal | Example Post | Outreach Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring for a role related to the problem | "Hiring our first RevOps manager" | Process is breaking and needs systems |
| Asking for tool recommendations | "What are people using for outbound?" | They are actively evaluating |
| Complaining about manual work | "Still doing this in spreadsheets" | Automation pain is visible |
| Announcing a launch | "We just launched our new product" | New GTM motion needs pipeline |
| Funding or growth announcement | "We raised our seed round" | New budget plus growth pressure |
| Competitor frustration | "Has anyone found an alternative to X?" | Switching window is open |

## Search Patterns

Use combinations like:

- `"any recommendations" + [tool category]`
- `"looking for" + [role or tool]`
- `"manual" + [workflow]`
- `"spreadsheet" + [workflow]`
- `"we just launched" + [category]`
- `"hiring" + [role] + [industry]`
- `"frustrated with" + [competitor]`

## Message Formula

```text
Saw your post about [specific signal].

Usually when [signal] happens, the annoying part is [specific pain].

I put together [asset or idea] that might help:
[link or concise advice]

Worth sending over?
```

## Disqualifiers

- No clear connection to the problem
- Student or job seeker when the offer needs a buyer
- Engagement bait with no real business context
- Too early, no company, no role, no budget
- No evidence of urgency
