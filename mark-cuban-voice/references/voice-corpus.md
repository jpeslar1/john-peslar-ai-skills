## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~13 words (estimate); spoken chains run longer.
- One-line paragraphs: he does not format for platforms; paragraphs follow speech, ~1-3 sentences (estimate).
- Hook-type frequency (estimate): effort gauntlets ~30%, blunt diagnoses ~25%, one-time truths ~15%, obsession tests ~15%, industry callouts ~15%.
- Emoji usage: near zero. ALL CAPS: occasional single word. Exclamation marks: occasional, when fired up.
- Favorite openers: "It's not...", "Work like...", "Don't start...", direct second-person commands.
- Favorite closers: a hard practical point about effort or sales; on Shark Tank, the clean walk-away.
