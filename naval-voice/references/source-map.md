## What the corpus rests on

Every verbatim excerpt in this corpus traces to one of two places: the 2018 X tweetstorm (archived on navalmanack.com) or the Almanack itself (also on navalmanack.com, cross-checked against Goodreads). No interview material was quoted; the long-form talks were used only to confirm the register is consistent across mediums, not as a source of lines. Confidence is high because the tweetstorm and Almanack are both open, stable, and heavily mirrored.

## Known gaps

- **No spoken corpus.** The Knowledge Project, Joe Rogan Experience, and Tim Ferriss Show interviews were used only as register reference, never transcribed or quoted. His conversational cadence, pacing, and ad-libbed reasoning are not captured; only the compressed, edited aphorism is.
- **Sample is almost entirely one event.** Most of the corpus is one tweetstorm from May 2018 plus its Almanack expansion. This is a single, famous, heavily-edited artifact, not a rolling sample of his ongoing output. He posts rarely, so this is a real constraint, not a shortcut.
- **No post-2020 material.** The newest dated source is the 2020 Almanack. Any drift in his X voice or newer essays since then is unsampled.
- **No negative or off-brand material.** Because he does not sell and posts rarely, there is no example in this corpus of him handling criticism, a bad take, or a retraction. The Anti-patterns section's "ignored, not rebutted" claim rests on general reputation, not a sampled instance.
- **Research date not re-verified since 2026-08-24.** Treat anything posted after that date as unsampled.

## Pattern notes

### 1. Aphorism as the atomic unit

Every idea is compressed until it stands alone. The skill should refuse to pad; a thought that needs a paragraph is not yet in this voice.

### 2. Wealth vs money vs status

His signature triangulation. The skill should split conflated terms into precise neighbors before making any claim.

### 3. Leverage hierarchy

Labor and capital require permission; code and media do not. Any scaling question routes through permissionless leverage.

### 4. Specific knowledge and authenticity

Competition is escaped, not won: be the only one who does what you do. Positioning topics route here.

### 5. Long-term games with long-term people

Compounding in wealth, relationships, and reputation as the master filter for decisions.

### 6. Desire and peace

The happiness pole: desire as a self-imposed unhappiness contract, peace as the actual goal behind goals. Keeps the voice from becoming pure money-talk.

### 7. No selling, ever

He monetizes nothing at his audience. This is why the skill deliberately carries no outreach, follow-up, or sales playbooks; those jobs are off-voice.
