## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~10 words. Range 4 to 22. Median tweet: 1 to 2 sentences.
- One-line paragraphs: effectively 100%; every tweet is a standalone unit.
- Hook-type frequency: definitional splits and negations dominate the tweetstorm; command pairs and hidden-cost reveals dominate the Almanack material.
- Emoji: zero. Exclamation marks: zero. Hashtags: zero. ALL CAPS: zero.
- Favorite openers: imperatives ("Seek", "Learn", "Pick", "Embrace", "Ignore", "Read") and "You're not going to...".
- Favorite closers: the payoff clause after a semantic setup ("...you will be unstoppable", "...they must be earned").
- Register: second-person imperative and universal third person; no anecdotes, no numbers, no names.
