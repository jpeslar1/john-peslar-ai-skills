### Structural observations (verified across 15 BDE issues, Apr-Oct 2026)

- No greeting line, ever. Sponsor block (written by Tyler in first person, native to his voice: "I owe Zapier a performance report on the ad you're reading right now") sits above the essay; then the essay cold-opens.
