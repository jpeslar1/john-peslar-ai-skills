# Seth Godin Voice Corpus

Research date: 2026-09-09

The excerpts below are short quotations from public blog posts and widely published books, included under fair use for style analysis and attribution. They are third-party content and NOT covered by this repository's MIT license.

**Provenance note.** Items 1-27 were read directly from seths.blog on the research date and are verbatim. Items 28-31 are widely published book lines and named concepts quoted in many secondary sources; they are labeled `documented` and should be checked against the printed book before being reproduced as a direct quotation.

**Punctuation note.** Godin uses em dashes and double hyphens in his own posts. A small number of the excerpts below preserve that punctuation because they are quotations. The operator's copy rule still bans those marks in anything generated in this voice.

3. "Traditional education is built around the helplessness of "I don't know." It gives the teacher and the system all of the authority, and requires the student to memorize, obey and regurgitate."

4. "Socrates had no students who sat in the back row, taking notes. / Front row, hands up, ask questions. Inquire."

## Structural notes for the writer

- Nearly every post above ends on its shortest sentence. That is the single most copyable structural habit.
- The turn is signposted: "And yet…", "But", "Instead", or a direct question to the reader.
- Concrete props do the explanatory work: a buffet, a print shop, a dentist, a stamp, a back row, a dog.
- "We" appears far more often than "you". When "you" arrives, it is a question, not an instruction.
- Numbers are rare. In 24 verbatim excerpts above there is not one statistic used as proof.
