## Known gaps

- **Book text was not fetched.** Items 28 to 31 in the corpus come from widely republished secondary quotation. They are marked `documented` and must be checked against the printed page before being used as a direct quote.
- **Sample is recent-heavy.** All verbatim excerpts are from a two-month window in 2026. His structure has been stable for twenty years, but a refresh should pull from 2010 and 2018 as well to confirm that the shape held.
- **The long-form register is under-sampled.** Akimbo episodes and book chapters run longer and argue harder than the blog. If a user asks for a 2000-word essay in this voice, sample the podcast first.
- **No social corpus.** He does not maintain an active personal posting presence on X or LinkedIn in the way the Tier 3 creators do. The blog is the channel.
