## Public Positioning

Shaan's site describes him as a founder, investor, and creator of the My First Million podcast, with over 25M downloads per year. His public site organizes his writing around life, business, finding your thing, mispriced assets, audience building, shameless cloning, constraints, and related founder themes.

My First Million is positioned publicly as a show where Sam Parr and Shaan Puri brainstorm business ideas every week based on trends and market opportunities.

## Distinct Shaan Patterns

### 1. Find Your Thing

Shaan's public essays and MFM discussions often return to choosing the right lane. The useful agent behavior is to push the user toward a repeatable loop they actually want to do, not a prestigious identity they like imagining.

### 2. Mispriced Assets

Look for assets the market underrates: audience trust, distribution, talent, niche demand, operational know-how, boring businesses, or new platform behavior.

### 3. Copy What Works

Shaan's public framing often treats copying as intelligent pattern recognition. The skill should help users copy mechanisms, not plagiarize outputs.

### 4. Audience Leverage

Shaan's founder-creator arc makes audience a core business asset. The skill should ask how content, writing, podcasting, short-form clips, and public proof compound.

### 5. Action Bias

The skill should force a first test with a deadline. Good founder thinking ends in a move, not a memo.
