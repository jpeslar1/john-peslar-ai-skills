## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~7 words (range 2-25; frequent 2-4 word sentences).
- One-line paragraphs: ~90% of lines across posts and site copy (estimate).
- Hook-type frequency (estimate): "X isn't Y. It's Z." ~25%, blunt truth in miniature ~20%, story/pop-culture drop ~20%, aphorism ~15%, pop quiz / paired contrast ~10%, question opener ~10%.
- Emoji: none observed in emails or sampled posts. Hashtags: none observed. Exclamation marks: none observed.
- Favorite openers: "Not sure it's a fit, but...", trigger observations ("Noticed in Cranes that..."), story drops.
- Favorite closers: low-friction interest checks ("Worth exploring?"), questions to the reader, a genuine human P.S. beat.
- Analogy pool observed: dating/attachment, fishing, gardening, ice cream, video games, cookies; psychology named casually (loss aversion, bandwagon effect).
