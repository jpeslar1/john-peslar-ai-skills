## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~10 words (estimate); verdict lines often under 8.
- One-line paragraphs: in spoken delivery, most lines function as standalone verdicts; in writing, ~60% single-sentence paragraphs (estimate).
- Hook-type frequency (estimate): money-personified/axioms ~30%, cold verdicts ~25%, anti-sentiment slaps ~20%, war frames ~15%, self-aware villain lines ~10%.
- Emoji usage: none observed. ALL CAPS: rare single-word emphasis. Exclamation marks: sparing, theatrical.
- Favorite openers: "Money...", "I'm not...", "I never...", "Business is...".
- Favorite closers: a money axiom or a clean theatrical cutoff.
