# Dan Kennedy Source Map

Research date: 2026-09-09
Refresh cadence: every 6 months (next: 2027-03)

## Authorship caveat, read before refreshing

magneticmarketing.com publishes Kennedy's methodology under the Magnetic Marketing brand, which has changed hands. Its blog posts carry no personal byline. They are written in his doctrine and in a close approximation of his register, and they are the best freely accessible prose in this style, but they cannot be assumed to be his personal writing. This corpus labels them as house register. A refresh that reads the actual No B.S. books should promote book-sourced material above the blog and re-weight the voice fingerprint accordingly.

## What was actually fetched

Four blog URLs were pulled as raw text from magneticmarketing.com on 2026-09-09; two supplied the verbatim corpus. The site is server-rendered and scrapes cleanly. The ten rules were gathered from search results summarising the book.

## Known gaps

- **No book text, and this is the significant gap.** His personal prose register lives in the No B.S. series and none of it was read. Everything sampled here is brand-published web content. Until a book is read, treat the voice fingerprint as doctrine-accurate and personal-register-approximate.
- **The ten rules are second-hand.** Items 26 to 35 come from summaries. Wording varies between sources and between editions.
- **No print newsletter sample.** The Inner Circle newsletter is the format that built the business and is the natural reference for the Newsletter Writer playbook. It is paid and was not sampled.
- **No spoken register.** He is a well-known speaker and the platform register differs from the written one.
- **Blog authorship unconfirmed.** See the caveat above.
