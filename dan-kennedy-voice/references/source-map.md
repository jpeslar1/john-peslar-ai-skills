## Authorship caveat, read before refreshing

magneticmarketing.com publishes Kennedy's methodology under the Magnetic Marketing brand, which has changed hands. Its blog posts carry no personal byline. They are written in his doctrine and in a close approximation of his register, and they are the best freely accessible prose in this style, but they cannot be assumed to be his personal writing. This corpus labels them `documented`, not `verified`, and calls them house register throughout. As of this revision, dankennedy.com 301-redirects to magneticmarketing.com, confirming the two are now the same operation, which reinforces rather than resolves the caveat.

**This gap is now partly closed, not fully closed.** Items 36-49 in the corpus are `verified`: personally bylined entrepreneur.com pieces, two of them explicit excerpts from his own named books. See "What was fetched on 2026-09-09" below. On 2026-10-19 items 101-130 added thirty passages of book prose from three No B.S. titles via publisher-framed entrepreneur.com excerpts; see "What was fetched on 2026-10-19". The house register (items 1-25) is unchanged and still unconfirmed as his personal writing.

## Known gaps

- **The house register (items 1-25) is still unconfirmed as personal writing.** This revision did not change that; it made the label honest instead of leaving it implied. Closing this gap fully means either the blog begins carrying personal bylines, or it gets dropped in favor of confirmed sources.
- **The ten rules (items 26-35) are partly matched, not confirmed.** Rules 2-6 (items 27-31) now point to his own book prose that states each rule in substance: urgency, clear instructions, tracking, brand as a by-product, and follow-up (items 101-120). The excerpts do not print the numbered "Rule N:" wording, so the list text is still second-hand. Rules 1 (always an offer) and 7-10 (strong copy, mail-order look, results rule, direct marketing diet) have no match in anything fetched.
- **No full book was read cover to cover.** Coverage is now publisher-chosen excerpts, not whole books. For No B.S. Direct Marketing (third edition) that is eight chapter-length excerpts on rules 2-6 plus follow-up and message/market/media; items 101-124 quote seven of them. For No B.S. Marketing to the Affluent and No B.S. Time Management there are one and two passages each in the corpus (items 125-130), and The Best of No B.S. is still a single excerpt (items 39-44). Not covered at all: No B.S. Price Strategy, the offer chapter (rule 1), and the chapters behind rules 7-10. Entrepreneur's editors chose what to excerpt, and the 2013 excerpts are marked "edited".
- **No print newsletter sample.** The Inner Circle newsletter is the format that built the business and is the natural reference for the Newsletter Writer playbook. It is paid and was not sampled.
- **No spoken register.** He is a well-known speaker and the platform register differs from the written one. YouTube transcript access was blocked this session (see above); this gap is unchanged from the prior revision, not newly discovered.
- **Thirteen of about 73 bylined pages quoted.** The entrepreneur.com author index lists about 73 pages; 13 are now quoted (3 on 2026-09-09, 10 on 2026-10-19). Unquoted excerpts from Ruthless Management, Brand-Building by Direct Response, Sales Success, and further Marketing to the Affluent and Time Management pages remain available at low cost.
