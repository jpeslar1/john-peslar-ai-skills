# John Peslar AI Skills

Free agent skills for founders, operators, marketers, and builders who want AI to help them think better, sell better, and automate real business workflows.

These skills work with agents that understand the open `SKILL.md` pattern, including Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, and other agentskills-compatible tools.

## Why This Exists

Most people use AI as a better text box. The real leverage comes from giving agents repeatable operating systems: strategy lenses, workflow maps, offer frameworks, lead generation playbooks, and content engines that can be reused every week.

This repo is the public skill library from John Peslar AI. It gives away practical systems that John uses across AI automation, lead generation, content, and founder strategy.

## Install

## Skill Details

### [`chamath-strategy-coach`](chamath-strategy-coach/SKILL.md)

Chamath-inspired strategy coaching for business decisions, capital allocation, founder operating systems, ambition, consistency, and hard-truth planning. Use it when you need a direct read on what compounds, what is status-seeking, what the real bottleneck is, and what should happen next.

**Skill author:** John Peslar AI ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@JohnPeslarAi), [X](https://x.com/johnwhereareu))

**Source inspiration:** [Chamath Palihapitiya](https://x.com/chamath) and his public YouTube channel, including videos on business advice, investing, elite performance, risk, status, and consistency. This skill is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chamath Palihapitiya.

**Use when you need to:**

- Pressure-test a business plan or strategy session
- Decide whether a spend, hire, or project is worth it
- Find the actual bottleneck in a growth plan
- Separate compounding assets from status games
- Turn ambition into an operating cadence
- Review a risky bet without getting swept up in hype
- Ask the blunt questions a founder is avoiding

**Example prompts:**

```text
Use $chamath-strategy-coach to pressure-test my plan to spend $1,000/month on a YouTube editor.
```

```text
Use $chamath-strategy-coach to audit this annual plan and tell me what is real strategy versus wishful thinking.
```

```text
Use $chamath-strategy-coach to help me build a personal operating system for ambition, focus, and consistency.
```

- Decide what should be automated, assisted, approved, or left alone
- Create a first version of an AI agent spec
- Map triggers, inputs, tools, outputs, and review gates
- Create an SOP for repeatable AI-assisted work
- Audit an existing process for time savings
- Build a 2-week automation implementation plan

**Example prompts:**

```text

```

```text
Use $ai-workflow-architect to audit this SOP and show me where AI can save time without reducing quality.
```


- Turn a webinar or post into a gated asset

### [`offer-stack-builder`](offer-stack-builder/SKILL.md)

Offer design skill for packaging services, SaaS products, AI automation offers, coaching, communities, and productized services. Use it to clarify the buyer, promise, mechanism, deliverables, proof, bonuses, guarantee, pricing logic, and next step.

**Skill author:** John Peslar AI ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@JohnPeslarAi), [X](https://x.com/johnwhereareu))

**Based on:** John's offer-building work across AI automation, lead generation, community, and productized service offers. This skill is designed to make offers easier to understand, easier to buy, and easier to deliver.

**Use when you need to:**

- Improve an offer that is not converting
- Turn a custom service into a productized package
- Add useful bonuses without fake value stacking
- Design a risk reversal or guarantee
- Clarify pricing logic
- Rewrite the offer promise
- Build a landing page outline from the offer

**Example prompts:**

```text

```

```text

```
