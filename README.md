# John Peslar AI Skills

Free agent skills for founders, operators, marketers, and builders who want AI to help them think better, sell better, and automate real business workflows.

These skills work with agents that understand the open `SKILL.md` pattern, including Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, and other agentskills-compatible tools.

## Why This Exists

Most people use AI as a better text box. The real leverage comes from giving agents repeatable operating systems: strategy lenses, workflow maps, offer frameworks, lead generation playbooks, and content engines that can be reused every week.

This repo is the public skill library from John Peslar AI. It gives away practical systems that John uses across AI automation, lead generation, content, and founder strategy.
