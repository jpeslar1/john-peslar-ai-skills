## Derived stats (honest estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~15 words in posts; the rule-posts are often a single sentence of 8-20 words, and announcement posts stack one-line paragraphs.
- One-line paragraphs: nearly every paragraph in the 2025-2026 long posts is one line; thread posts are one or two sentences each.
- Hook-type frequency across the 29 posts: conditional rule with blunt consequence ~35%, specific number as proof ~25%, announcement formula ("Ladies and gentlemen", "Sold my company") ~15%, deadpan candor or spite ~15%, mapping list (Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack) ~10%.
- Hedges: effectively zero in posts (no "I think", no "maybe"); the only self-undercut is the thread closer telling readers to distrust the list.
- Numbers: at least one in roughly 60% of posts (7 adult friends, 7 new people per session, 48 hours, $500, No. 78, 10 years, 5 years, 3 seconds, 40%); always specific, never rounded to "many" or "most".
- Emoji: one in 29 posts (the thread-opener pointer); arrows used as mapping glyphs once. Hashtags: zero.
- Caps for emphasis: rare and structural (TIME FOR A THREAD, DEFCON 5, Dead On Arrival, Aha Moment as proper nouns).
- Profanity: zero. Insults: present but flat ("the morons at the New York Times", "Your entire account is repost factory").
- Scare quotes on distrusted words: "partnership", "creator", "cold start", "meetup with friends", "escape velocity" (the last used approvingly).
- Ampersands replace "and" in roughly a third of thread posts to save characters.
- Favorite openers: "If your product...", a number or a date ("In 2014", "Sold my company 5 years ago"), "Ladies and gentlemen", "Here's a window into how I approach...".
- Favorite closers: a blunt one-clause verdict ("run.", "it's over.", "It was strengthening X."), a wink sign-off ("See you on the Timeline"), or a warning to distrust the advice just given.
- Spoken register (ASR sample): heavy "like", "I I" self-repair and "you know"; the same metaphors recur verbatim across spoken and written (every tap is a miracle, latent demand, 3 seconds, black swan), so posts read as the compressed form of the riffs.
