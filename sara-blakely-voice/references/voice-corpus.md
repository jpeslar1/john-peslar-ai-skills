# Sara Blakely Voice Corpus

Research date: 2026-10-18

The excerpts below are short quotations from public posts, articles, and talks, included under fair use for style analysis and attribution. They are third-party content and NOT covered by this repository's MIT license.

**Provenance note.** Items 1-25 are verbatim: copied from the full text of her own public LinkedIn posts (profile sarablakely27), which render server-side without login; dates were decoded from each post's activity ID (a millisecond timestamp), accurate to the day. Items 26-33 are DOCUMENTED: YouTube auto-captions (ASR) of two long interviews (Stanford GSB View From The Top; Forbes), with punctuation added and no words changed; one caption slip is flagged inline. Nothing here comes from a quote aggregator. Her Instagram, her highest-volume channel, is login-walled and was not fetched; the LinkedIn posts are largely the same captions cross-posted. Emoji inside quotes are hers.

**Punctuation note.** One excerpt (item 23) preserves an en dash because it is a verbatim quotation; it is an outlier in a corpus that runs on ellipses and exclamation marks. The operator's copy rule still bans em and en dashes in anything generated in this voice; see the Punctuation Override in SKILL.md.

## Derived stats (honest estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~12 words in posts; spoken answers run longer with 'you know' and 'I always say' as connectors.
- One-line paragraphs: roughly a third of post lines; most posts are 2-4 short paragraphs.
- Hook-type frequency across the 25 written posts: prop scene ~35%, anniversary count ~20%, quoted pressure ~15%, ridiculous confession ~15%, reader question ~15%.
- Ellipses: 2-6 per post, mid-sentence, as pauses or drumrolls.
- Exclamation marks: 2-8 per post, multiplied at peaks ("today!!!!!", "BOOTS!!!").
- Emoji: 1-3 per post, almost always at line end; set = 👊 😳 😂 ❤️ 🥂 🏔️ 👍 🌟.
- Caps for emphasis: one or two words per post (HUGE, A LOT, BOOTS).
- Hashtags: 2-4 at the end of most LinkedIn posts, including a series tag (#MondayMugshot, #TBT, #GetRidiculous).
- Numbers: one round anecdote figure per post at most ($5,000, 21 years, $10,000); no percentages.
- Profanity: zero. Named critics: zero.
- Favorite openers: a prop in a scene; an anniversary count; what people kept saying, in quotes.
- Favorite closers: a one- or two-line imperative moral ("Start. Jump. The world needs you." / "Define yourself!") plus an emoji, or a question to readers; brand letters signed "Xo, Sara Blakely".
- Spoken register (ASR sample): 'belief system', 'gut and intuition', 'I always say', the fax-machine years as the recurring anchor; humor stated as a tactic ('a laugh buys 30 seconds').
