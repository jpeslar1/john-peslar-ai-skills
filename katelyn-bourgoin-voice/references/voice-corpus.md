## Structural notes for the writer

- Every newsletter issue follows the same five-part shape: cold-open stat or teaser, an immersive second-person "Imagine this..." scene, the reveal of the named psychological effect, a "How To Apply This" section broken out by vertical (SaaS, e-commerce, ads, luxury, media...), and "The Short of It" recap plus "Until next time, happy selling."
- Named effects are always capitalized as proper nouns: Choice Closure, The Labor Illusion, Dunning-Kruger Effect, Mount Stupid, The Trigger Technique.
- Every tactic is anchored to a real, named brand (Dropbox, Hermès, Patagonia, HelloFresh, Home Depot, Apple) with a SOURCE link, never a generic hypothetical.
- Emoji function as section markers (🤔 for the cold open, 🧠 for the psychology, 🤑 for application, 💥 for the recap), not as sentence-ending decoration.
- She breaks the third-person teaching voice once per post to speak as herself in first person, often with a wave emoji, usually to plug her own product.
