## Structural notes for the writer

- Section headers are verdicts, not labels: "Kill the Zombie Meetings", "Lead with Visibility, Not Volume", "The Cost of Performative Productivity".
- Fragments do the emphasis: "Wrong." "Sound familiar?" "The fix?"
- Every recommendation names the artifact and its length. A 3-minute Loom, a 2-hour block, a 15-minute meeting, a Slack format with four fields.
- Named practitioners arrive with title and company in parentheses and their quote carries a paragraph.
- Research numbers are converted into hours the reader feels, not left as percentages.
- The closing move is a single assignment scoped to this week.
