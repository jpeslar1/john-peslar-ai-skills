## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~9 words. Range 3 to 24.
- One-line paragraphs: ~70% of social-post lines are standalone.
- Hook-type frequency (her viral posts): absurd comparison and timeline flex dominate (~half), then confession-then-reveal, then list promise.
- Numbers: nearly every hook carries at least one dollar figure, age, or count.
- Emoji: rare to none in top posts. ALL CAPS: single-word emphasis only (MINE).
- Favorite openers: "At [age]...", "I thought...", "[Sexy thing]: [bad math]. My [boring thing]: [good math]."
- Favorite closers: colon-launched list promises, smallest-first-step CTAs, mission statements about ownership.
- Register: blunt, first person, direct address, mild profanity roughly once per long piece.
