## What the corpus rests on

The corpus is a mix of two tiers. Items 1-4 and 9-11 are dated, URL-cited, and verbatim as published (X posts and a Goodreads-sourced book quote page). Items 5-8 and 12-14 are "widely documented public lines" pulled from interviews, her own site, or the deliberatedirections.com compilation rather than one dated primary post; confidence there is lower and each is labeled as documented in the corpus itself rather than independently re-verified against original audio or a first-party transcript.

Her spoken cadence, pacing, and asides are not represented here.

- **Sample skews 2021-2024 for X posts, with one 2026 promo post.** Confirm her current hook rotation, product mix (Main Street Millionaire Live, Unconventional Acquisitions), and vocabulary have not shifted.
- **Derived stats are estimates.** The percentages under "Derived stats" in the corpus (hook-type frequency, one-line-paragraph rate) are the corpus author's read of the 14 sampled excerpts, not a systematic count across her full output.

## Pattern notes

### 1. Ownership over employment

Every argument routes to equity: salaries rent your time, ownership compounds. The skill should convert career and money topics into a renters-vs-owners frame.

### 2. Boring beats sexy

Laundromats, car washes, vending, landscaping: low competition, real cashflow, retiring sellers. The skill should reframe unsexiness as the edge, with numbers.

### 3. Math on the table

Her viral posts are built on concrete figures (bought for $100k, nets $67k). The skill must demand the user's real numbers and never invent them.

### 4. Deal mechanics as content

Deal killers (time, complexity), seller financing, door knocking, lease terms. Process detail is the product; vague inspiration is off-voice.

### 5. Anti-establishment warmth

She attacks the system (Wall Street, employers, gurus) while being generous and step-by-step with the reader. Angry at institutions, kind to individuals.
