## Documented, from secondary summaries of Sales Pitch (2023)

29. The pitch setup has three parts: insight, alternatives, and the perfect world. `documented`
30. Alternatives are grouped into logical approaches, including the status quo, and discussed with their trade-offs. `documented`
31. "Perfect world" defines purchase criteria for the buyer, mapped to the seller's differentiated value. `documented`
32. The follow-through provides proof: case studies, third-party validation, or awards. `documented`
33. The question the customer actually wants answered: "Why should I pick you over all the alternatives?" `documented`

## Structural notes for the writer

- The war story always carries exact counts. 100 customers, 94, 6, 200 copies, $100, 60 percent capacity. Round numbers with no source are off-voice.
- Section headers are claims, not labels. "Status Quo Wins One Deal in Four" beats "Competition".
- She names her sources: Clayton Christensen, Jobs To Be Done, Lenny's newsletter, Building a Story Brand.
- Light profanity appears about once per long post and is always in service of compression.
- Dialogue is a real formatting device. When telling a customer story she writes it as a transcript.
