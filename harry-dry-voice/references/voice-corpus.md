# Harry Dry Voice Corpus

Research date: 2026-09-09

The excerpts below are short quotations from public pages and interviews, included under fair use for style analysis and attribution. They are third-party content and NOT covered by this repository's MIT license.

**Fidelity warning.** marketingexamples.com renders its body copy client-side, so the pages could not be scraped as raw text. Items below were read through a page-reading tool that returns the page's own wording for headings and quoted lines but summarises surrounding prose. Rule names and quoted sentences are reliable; anything longer than a sentence should be re-checked on the live page before being republished as a direct quotation. Items 28-31 come from a third-party interview writeup and are labeled accordingly, and item 33 is a line by Blair Warren that Harry Dry features.

## Attributed to others, quoted by him

33. "People will do anything for those who encourage their dreams, justify their failures, allay their fears, confirm their suspicions, and help throw rocks at their enemies"
    This is **Blair Warren's** line, featured on https://marketingexamples.com/copywriting/psychology-of-persuasion as "27 words that sum up the psychology of copywriting". Attribute to Warren, never to Dry.

## Structural notes for the writer

- Every rule name is two to four words and starts with a verb or a flat negative.
- The teaching unit is rule, bad line, good line. There is no theory paragraph between them.
- Real brands carry the argument. Etsy against Amazon Handmade, New Balance, Red Bull, DuckDuckGo, Lane Bryant, Feastables, Sky Bet.
- British spellings in his own copy: visualise, favourite, appetising.
- Mild profanity appears once in a while as compression ("You can't bullshit specifics"), never as heat.
