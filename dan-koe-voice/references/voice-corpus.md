## Structural notes for the writer

- Chains of consequence are the signature structure. One link per line, arrow or plain, building an argument that feels inevitable.
- The rhythm is dense-then-land: three or four conceptual sentences, then a single short line that resolves them.
- Section headers are compressed theses of three to six words, never labels.
- Anaphora arrives in runs of three, never two and never four.
- Quotes from named thinkers open sections and are always extended, never left as decoration.
- The essay always descends from philosophy into a literal instruction with a time frame.
- Profanity is asterisked in headers and brand copy, unasterisked and rare in body prose.

**Spoken register (added 2026-10-18 from items 101+):**
- Cadence: longer, looser sentences chained with "and so", "so", "because", "in other words"; the dense-then-land rhythm survives but the landing is often spoken twice. Section headers become signposts ("So now we're going to learn…", "Now the second step is…").
- Openers: a numbered self-diagnosis ("I want you to tell me if any of these sound like you. 1. …") or a cascade of six to eight questions the viewer is already asking; the one idea is named within 90 seconds.
- Closers: the philosophical idea descends into a numbered procedure, then an explicit, unembarrassed handoff to the product (Eden, the bootcamp, "link in the description"), then like-and-subscribe and a trailer.
- Spoken pet phrases: "That's kind of not cool", "I guess", "so to say", "It just makes sense, right?", "literally", "good dopamine", "psychic entropy", "point of view", "reality distortion field", "value creator, not a manipulator", "problem, transformation, solution". Named thinkers still open sections (Musk, Maltz, de Mello, Priestley, Schwartz).
- Tangents are allowed, then explicitly closed ("Well, let's get back to the video"); the letters never do this.
- Spoken vs written: profanity plainer (one unasterisked "shit"), hedges appear ("I guess", "kind of"), identity chains are narrated as cause and effect rather than one link per line, receipts are more specific (5,000 followers, $100K a month) and friends are named.
