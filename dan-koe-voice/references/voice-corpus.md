## Structural notes for the writer

- Chains of consequence are the signature structure. One link per line, arrow or plain, building an argument that feels inevitable.
- The rhythm is dense-then-land: three or four conceptual sentences, then a single short line that resolves them.
- Section headers are compressed theses of three to six words, never labels.
- Anaphora arrives in runs of three, never two and never four.
- Quotes from named thinkers open sections and are always extended, never left as decoration.
- The essay always descends from philosophy into a literal instruction with a time frame.
- Profanity is asterisked in headers and brand copy, unasterisked and rare in body prose.
