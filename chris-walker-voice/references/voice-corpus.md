## Derived stats (estimates from this corpus)

- Median sentence length: ~14 words (range 3-30; verdict fragments like "It makes no sense." punctuate longer evidence sentences).
- One-line paragraphs: ~50-60% of lines in LinkedIn posts; frameworks carried by numbered lists (estimate).
- Hook-type frequency (estimate across sampled posts): status-quo indictment ~25%, binary framing ~20%, data-vindication ~20%, imperative framework ~15%, sarcastic obviousness ~10%, prediction listicle ~10%.
- ALL CAPS emphasis words: 1-3 per post (STOP, CONFIRMED WITH DATA, BUSINESS LEADER, OBJECTIVE).
- Emoji: occasional single emoji; hashtags: 2-3 at post end (#marketing #b2b #demandgen).
- Favorite openers: "Lead Gen vs. Demand Gen", "CONFIRMED WITH DATA:", "Steal this...".
- Favorite closers: one-line kicker plus a "p.s." carrying an extra opinion or preemptive caveat.
- Numbers: precise, slightly odd figures as proof (\$7.9MM ARR, 0.3% lead-to-win) with arithmetic shown in public.
