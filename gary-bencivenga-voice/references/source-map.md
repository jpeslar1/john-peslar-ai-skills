## What the corpus rests on

All thirty-five excerpts are verbatim from the one channel he writes himself: thirty-two from Bencivenga Bullets, his complimentary e-letter of 29 issues (c. 2004-2008), read as Wayback captures of the original static pages (26 of 29 issues pulled, 12 quoted, plus the standing sign-off), and three from the live marketingbullets.com homepage, his current Marketing Maxims letter. Zero items are documented-grade; nothing comes from a quote aggregator or a secondary compilation. Because he has no social channels and no public spoken record, the corpus is a single written register with a current-era coda, which is structural rather than a research shortfall. Confidence in wording is high throughout; the one caveat is that individual Bullets carry no dates and are bracketed only by internal evidence.

## Known gaps

- **No dates on individual Bullets.** The series is bracketed by Bullet #23 opening on Gary Halbert's death (April 2007), Bullet #22 citing Bob Parsons' rules "copyright 2004-2007", and every footer reading "Copyright 2008, Accountable Advertising, Inc." Wayback's earliest captures of marketingbullets.com would narrow the start year; the CDX query was limited to 2012-2016 this session.
- **Only written text.** His spoken voice (the 2005 "Bencivenga 100" farewell seminar, interviews with Brian Kurtz or Ken McCarthy) is paid or not publicly transcribed; nothing spoken was retrievable without API spend. Any claim about how he speaks is unsupported.
- **His actual control copy is absent.** The Boardroom, Phillips and Rodale magalogs and the Charles Givens "20 minutes a month" package are quoted inside the Bullets only as headlines, never as full letters. The skill can quote his doctrine but not his ad copy.
- **Seventeen Bullets read or located but not quoted.** #4, #6-8, #10-11, #13-14, #17-18, #20-22, #24, #26, #28 were fetched or located in the CDX index and not quoted because their themes overlap the quoted issues; #20, #21 and #26 were located but not downloaded this session.
- **His results are self-reported.** "Tens of millions of dollars in scientific direct response tests" and "thousands of split-run tests" are his account; no independent audit exists.
- **No social channels exist** (no X, no LinkedIn posting), so the two-source footing cannot be widened by a refresh.
- **Anything published after 2026-10-19 is unsampled.** For this author that means a change to the Maxims homepage or a newly surfaced interview or seminar transcript.

## Pattern notes

### 1. Claim and proof joined at the hip
"Never make your claim bigger than your proof. And always join your claim and your proof at the hip in your headlines." The Objection Handling Expert shrinks the claim to its proof as step two and the Hook Creator forbids a headline claim without its proof beside it.

### 2. The withheld winner
A split-run is posed ("Which Headline Pulled Best?"), the principle is taught, then the winner and its number are revealed ("handily beat the previous champ"). The Ghostwriter builds its body on this delay.

### 3. Name the device
Every lesson gets a handle or a formula (the Credo Technique, the Red Shirts, Fuzzy Dice, I = B + C) because "you can easily remember a short, simple sentence." The Ghostwriter names one device per piece; the Hook Creator runs the I = B + C check on every headline.

### 4. Parable, then one-line moral
Tom Collins's sign, the red shirts, the horse and jockey, aspirin and headache, each closing on a line under ten words ("intention facilitates perception"). All three playbooks end on a maxim.

### 5. Why you, why true, why now
The reason-why checklist from the Bullet #9 poem. The Objection Handling Expert answers all three in sequence; the Ghostwriter places them after the reveal.

### 6. Candor, courtesy, and borrowed authority
Make the requirement moderately hard, admit the limitation, cite Hopkins or Caples before your own rule, and route self-praise through others ("allow me to quote what others have said about my work"). The Objection Handling Expert uses candor as proof and the Emotional register fix re-routes any boast through a quoted third party.
