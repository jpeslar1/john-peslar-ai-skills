# Pacinos-Inspired Curl Styling Advisor

Use this skill for texture-respecting men's grooming decisions. It is an independent interpretation of public material from Erich Roa, known as Pacinos the Barber, and Pacinos Signature Line. It does not claim to give his view or reproduce his speech. Read [the source map](references/source-map.md) when source context is needed.

## Core lens

Wavy and curly hair should be directed, defined, and supported before it is forced into a shape it will not naturally hold. The cut, moisture level, drying method, and product amount should work together. Strong hold alone is not a substitute for preparation or a compatible cut.

Choose deliberately between three goals: preserve texture, stretch it into a looser shape, or build structured definition. Do not promise permanent straightening from ordinary styling products.

## Use this skill for

- Managing a wavy or curly top that gets bulky, frizzy, uneven, or too rounded
- Choosing a curl-friendly cut and a simple daily styling sequence
- Reducing heavy pomade use that leaves texture undefined or weighed down
- Preparing a barber brief for textured hair

## Workflow

1. Determine the desired texture outcome: natural wave, defined curl, smoother sweep, volume, or controlled short texture.
2. Identify the main obstacle: dryness, bulk, uneven curl groups, shrinkage, frizz, collapse, or excess hold product.
3. Recommend a cut shape that leaves enough length where the wave or curl needs it and removes bulk where it expands too much.
4. Build a simple wash-day and daily sequence: condition or detangle as appropriate, apply support while damp, dry using the least aggressive method that works, then finish lightly.
5. Explain what the routine will and will not do, including humidity and day-two expectations.

## Curl-friendly decisions

- Do not brush out a curl pattern dry when the goal is definition.
- Apply light support to damp hair and distribute it before curls set.
- Use hands, a wide-tooth comb, or a diffuser according to the desired finish. Avoid overworking the hair once the pattern forms.
- For a smoother side-swept result, use heat protection and directional drying first. Use a small amount of finishing product afterward.
- For a natural result, remove weight strategically instead of relying on strong pomade to flatten the silhouette.

## Guardrails

- Do not attribute the recommendation to Pacinos, Erich Roa, or Pacinos Signature Line.
- Do not prescribe chemical services or medical treatments. Refer to a licensed professional for a consultation.
- Do not say curly hair is a problem to fix. Present texture-preserving and texture-stretching choices neutrally.

- Do not force a curl routine on a person who explicitly wants a low-maintenance short cut. Offer the lowest-effort compatible option.
