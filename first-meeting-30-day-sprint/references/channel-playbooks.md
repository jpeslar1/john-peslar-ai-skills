# Channel playbooks

Read only the chosen route. These are practical expansions of Sales 101 curriculum pages 6-9. The outline supplies principles, not complete scripts or exact timings. Adapt the drafts to supported prospect facts. Never invent personalization, peer results, local knowledge, or a prior relationship.

## Warm route

Select people who actually know the seller or can credibly introduce them to the buyer. Draft a short update about the problem being explored and one relevant question or introduction request. For an untested ICP, ask about their experience rather than pretending the offer is validated.

Hypothetical structure: "I'm testing a small service for [buyer] dealing with [problem]. Is that something your team is dealing with, or do you know someone who is?" Replace every field from evidence before treating it as ready to send. If interest is real, clarify the problem and then suggest a brief discussion with a clear purpose.

## Cold email route

The curriculum names Josh Braun's 4T: Trigger, Think, Third-party, Talk. Draft one short email, and annotate the four parts separately for the user. Do not put the labels in the actual email.

- Trigger: a current, sourced observation relevant to the buyer's work.
- Think: a tentative connection to a possible problem, without claiming to know their private situation.
- Third-party: relevant and true external evidence or a real result the seller is allowed to use.
- Talk: one low-effort question to learn whether the problem exists.

If third-party proof is unavailable, omit the claim and explicitly mark the draft as a proof-light adaptation, not a completed evidence-backed 4T email. Offer a relevant demo only if it exists, labeled as a demo. Never fill this slot with "we helped dozens of teams" when the user has no clients.

Write an honest short subject, one idea, and a reply-first ask. Test on ten actual ICP matches after sending authorization. Inspect delivery and list quality before blaming copy for silence. Stop on refusal and do not claim research invitations are sales meetings.

## LinkedIn route

The curriculum names a blank connection request, four follow-up message slots, a human/personal final touch, and reply before call. Produce only the steps still relevant to the relationship.

1. Blank connection request if not connected. Do not pitch in the request.
2. Message 1 after acceptance: supported observation and one easy question.
3. Message 2 if no reply: a genuinely useful new angle or resource.
4. Message 3 if no reply: relevant proof or a helpful demonstration, clearly identified. Without proof, use a new relevant question or omit the touch; never fabricate a customer result.
5. Message 4 if no reply: a respectful personal close-out. A geographic reference is optional and must be true and relevant. Do not invent having visited their city or knowing their interests.

The blank request is separate from the four messages. A few business days between eligible touches is an adjustable planning default, not a timing rule supplied by Kara. Stop the remaining sequence on any reply; handle the response first. Stop on rejection or a no-contact request. After the final unanswered touch, park the record instead of adding indefinite follow-ups. Do not assert current platform limits or benchmark rates without checking reliable current sources.

## Magnet route

Use this when the seller can already reach relevant buyers. Create a shortcut for one buying problem: a checklist, calculator worksheet, annotated workflow, or diagnostic that a person can use quickly. Define the useful result, inputs, steps, and a worked hypothetical example. Complete the asset, not just its title or promotional post.

Write an invitation to receive it, a delivery message, and a later optional 15-minute application-call ask tied to the recipient's problem. Deliver the promised asset before requesting the call. Do not make the call an undisclosed condition for receiving it. Track recipients, actual delivery, replies, booked meetings, and held meetings. Downloads or comments alone are not sales outcomes.

## Move an interested reply to a meeting

First establish that the problem and buyer fit. Then propose a brief discussion with a clear purpose. Use actual available slots if known; otherwise draft a request for availability and leave the calendar action pending. Confirm date, time, timezone, attendees, and purpose from the buyer's reply. Do not report a booking until agreement exists.
